ABOUT

Who is behind MétisWorks

Métis is the Greek goddess of practical, resourceful intelligence: knowing what to do next, and doing it well.

I am Luisa. For twenty years I have worked where people, technology and business meet: digital product management at Vodafone, customer value management and loyalty consulting at Accenture, and ten years at Fjord, Accenture's design and innovation arm, as Design Lead and Director.

I have designed and launched services in telco, banking, insurance, life sciences and energy, across Switzerland, Italy, the UAE, Greece, Romania and Turkey. I built and co-led a design studio of 15 inside a telco, and led programmes from a first strategic question to launch. Engineering degree, MBA, and a habit of asking what a service is really for.

Design without a business case is a proposal. A business case without design is a spreadsheet. I work where the two meet, the only place things actually launch.

I started in engineering and customer value management, where every change had to show up in a number. Design taught me the other half: why people do what they do.
